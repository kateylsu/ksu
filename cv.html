Research Interests

  • Debris Disk Evolution Using spatially resolved imaging, spectral energy distributions, and infrared variability, I characterize dust properties and dynamical states across stellar ages - from young systems to white dwarfs - to trace how planetary architectures shape debris disk structure and evolution.
  • Planetary Systems I leverage debris disks as probes of planetary system formation and evolution. By analyzing infrared variability and disk emission, I identify dynamically active systems where ongoing planetary interactions sculpt circumstellar dust, revealing constraints on planetary architectures and system histories.
